# Skinalyze Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 26, 2026 (Skinalyze 2.0)*

Skinalyze is a skin-care habit tracker. It's built to keep your face, your scans and your health information on your
own iPhone. This policy explains what the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- Your face photos, scans and 3D face models stay on your iPhone and in **your own iCloud**. We can't see them.
- Apple Health data is **read on your iPhone only**, never stored by Skinalyze, and never sent anywhere.
- There are **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.
- If you join Friends and leagues, other people see a **generated name, your streak and your points**, never photos.

## What Skinalyze uses, and why

### Camera, photos and 3D scans
Skinalyze uses the front camera to take your scan photos. On iPhones with a TrueDepth camera, it also makes a 3D model of
your face. The spots and changes Skinalyze notices are worked out **on your iPhone**. Photos, scans, 3D models and your
results are saved on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on for Skinalyze, synced through **your private iCloud database**,
which only you can access. We don't receive them.

### Your profile and routine
Your skin type, goals, sensitivity, the products on your shelf, your routine, journal notes, streaks and points are
stored the same way: on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

### Apple Health (optional)
If you allow it, Skinalyze reads your **sleep** and **period days** from Apple Health to show them next to your scans on
the Patterns screen. This happens on your iPhone, only when you open that screen. Skinalyze doesn't store Health data,
doesn't write to Apple Health, and never sends Health data to iCloud, to us or to anyone else.

### Location (optional)
If you turn on the daily UV nudge, Skinalyze asks Apple's WeatherKit for today's UV level where you are, using
**approximate (reduced accuracy) location**. Your location is used for that request only. It isn't stored and isn't
shown to anyone.

### Friends and leagues (optional)
If you join, Skinalyze creates a small public profile in Apple's iCloud public database so friends and league members
can see it. It contains:

- a generated name (an adjective and an animal) and your invite code
- your streak, weekly points, league tier and room
- Pimpi's outfit, the day you were last active, and who invited you (their invite code)

It never contains photos, scans, skin results or Health data. You can stop sharing at any time (Friends > Stop
sharing), and your public profile is deleted. Because this profile is stored where we can technically access it, we list it in the App Store privacy
details as "User ID" and "Product Interaction", used only for app functionality.

### Product lookups
When you search for a product or scan a barcode, Skinalyze looks it up in its built-in catalog and, for some products,
in the public Open Beauty Facts database. Only the product search or barcode is sent, nothing about you.

### Purchases
Skinalyze Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we don't receive your payment details.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or share your data.
- We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
- No third-party AI service receives your photos or personal data.

## Your choices

- **Delete scans:** one at a time from the Deck (Delete scan), or all at once in Profile > Delete all scans. They're
  removed from your iPhone and your iCloud.
- **Delete everything:** delete the app, and remove Skinalyze's data under Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage
  Account Storage.
- **Health, location, camera:** turn them on or off any time in Settings > Privacy & Security.
- **Friends:** Friends > Stop sharing; your public profile is deleted.

## Children

Skinalyze isn't directed at children under 13.

## Not medical advice

Skinalyze tracks visible changes. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose. If something is painful, lasts a long
time or worries you, please see a dermatologist.
