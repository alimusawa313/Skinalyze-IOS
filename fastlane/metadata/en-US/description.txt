Meet Pimpi, your tiny skin-care buddy. Skinalyze helps you keep an eye on your skin with one quick scan a day, see how it changes over time, and figure out which products fit you, all without your photos ever leaving your iPhone.

ONE QUICK SCAN A DAY
Three angles, a few seconds, guided like setting up Face ID. Your scan turns into a little sticker for your deck. On iPhones with a TrueDepth camera, every scan also makes a 3D model of your face you can turn around.

EVERY SPOT, ON THE MAP
See the spots, breakouts and marks your scan picked up, placed on your photo and on the 3D model. Tap a dot to see what it is.

SEE WHAT CHANGED
Put any two days side by side, lined up by your eyes, and slide between them. Your deck keeps every scan in order, so you can look back whenever you like.

IS IT GOOD FOR ME?
Ask about any product: search, scan a barcode, read a label or share a page from Safari. Skinalyze checks its ingredients against your skin profile and tells you how well it fits, with the reasons. Build your morning and evening routine on your shelf, and Pimpi flags pairs that don't mix.

SHOW UP, EARN STREAKS
Streaks, daily quests, chests and outfits for Pimpi. Everything counts effort, never your skin: a rough skin week never costs you a thing.

TWO-MINUTE SKIN SCHOOL
Short, friendly lessons on routines, sunscreen, ingredients and spot myths. Learn a little, earn a little.

NOTICE PATTERNS
Jot down your day in a tap, and if you like, let Skinalyze read your sleep and cycle days from Apple Health. See what coincided with calmer or busier weeks. Health data is read on your iPhone only and never stored.

MORE TO LOVE
• Weekly recaps and a yearly Wrapped
• Friends and leagues that share streaks and points, never photos
• Widgets for Pimpi, your streak and a quick scan
• Made for every iPhone, including iPhone Duo, folded or open, upright or on its side
• Light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your photos, scans and 3D models stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, never on our servers. Nothing is shared unless you export it yourself.

Skinalyze tracks visible changes. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose. If something is painful, lasts a long time or worries you, consider seeing a dermatologist.

SKINALYZE PRO
The first scan of each day is always free. Skinalyze Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, billed weekly, monthly or yearly; the yearly plan starts with a 1-week free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it any time in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.
